A Sanctuary for the Soul

Located on the tranquil banks of the Kalu Hanga, our meditation center provides the perfect escape from the noise of daily life.

The gentle flow of the river and the rustling leaves create a natural symphony that aids in deep meditation and self-reflection. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced practitioner, our environment supports your path to inner peace.
